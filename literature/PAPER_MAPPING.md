### Research Context

These 23 papers formed the literature corpus analyzed in NotebookLM. Existing research has separately investigated RUL prediction under industrial sensor streams, statistical drift detection with adaptive retraining, and deployment-time mechanisms such as shadow or canary evaluation. However, the reviewed literature provides limited systematic evaluation of these mechanisms as an integrated, autonomous model-lifecycle for non-stationary physical prognostics, particularly with explicit validation and live shadow gating before model promotion.

**Key Research Domains:**
1. **Physical Predictive Maintenance** (Papers 2, 3, 12, 17, 22) — RUL prediction and prognostics in industrial systems
2. **Statistical ML Drift Management** (Papers 14, 18, 23) — Drift detection and adaptive retraining approaches
3. **Autonomics and Deployment Safety** (Papers 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 20) — Self-healing systems, shadow evaluation, and canary deployment patterns
